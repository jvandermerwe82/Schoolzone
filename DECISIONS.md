# SchoolZone — Durable Engineering Decisions

## Australia first

Australian Curriculum v9 is the active launch direction. Legacy England content may be retained for reuse but does not become Australian evidence without mapping.

## Deterministic learner state

Canonical learner progress, mastery, misconceptions and support evidence are owned by deterministic product logic/data contracts, not by generative tutor text.

## Cloud/server authority

When cloud mode is active, server state is authoritative. Browser/local state is resilience support.

## No diagnostic inference

Support adaptation may use observable learning/session evidence. SchoolZone does not diagnose or infer medical/neurodevelopmental conditions from behavior.

## Lab is not proof

Brain Lab synthetic/controlled results are regression evidence, not proof of educational efficacy or real-user benefit.

## Pilot before scale

Two-child controlled proof precedes broader cohort claims, school-scale rollout or government/curriculum efficacy claims.
